A week of Wikipedia traffic, sliced in seconds

Wikimedia counts every page view by project, page, access method and hour. This page holds one week of it, tens of millions of hourly rows in a single file, and asks DuckDB questions of it live in your browser: pick a time window, rank the pages, group by project or flag the hours a page jumped above its own trailing 24-hour average. Every step is a new query.
